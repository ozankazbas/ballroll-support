BallRoll Support

Need help with BallRoll, the minimalist sliding puzzle game? Contact support.nunurun@gmail.com.

Quick troubleshooting

  • If the app stops responding, close it and reopen it.
  • Check the Sound button on Home if you cannot hear effects.
  • Continue resumes your saved level from its starting board. Moves within an unfinished level are not saved.
  • Progress is stored on your device. Deleting the app may delete your saved progress.

When contacting support, describe the issue and, if useful, your app version and iPhone model. Do not send passwords or sensitive information.
