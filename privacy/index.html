BallRoll Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026.

BallRoll does not collect personal data through the app. There are no user accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Local game data

Game progress and sound preferences are stored locally on your device. BallRoll does not transmit this local progress or preference data to a server. No data is sold or shared with advertisers.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support.nunurun@gmail.com. If you choose to email support, the information you provide is used to respond to your request.

This website

These public support pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection information needed to serve the website under its privacy statement. This is separate from the BallRoll app, which does not send gameplay data to this website.
